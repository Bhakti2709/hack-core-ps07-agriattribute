Slide 02 — Detailed Solution & Problem Fit

From Uncertainty to Evidence

Solving Syngenta's "Seeing is Believing" Mandate
❌ The Problem
Farmers struggle to determine how much yield improvement is attributable to biological products vs. confounding factors like good monsoon rain, soil organic carbon, or favorable temperature.

Consequence: Lack of clear attribution reduces confidence, leading to low adoption & hesitation to reinvest in biostimulants.
✅ Our Solution Approach
AgriAttribute AI builds a Digital Twin of the farm using XGBoost & SHAP. It compares the expected yield WITH Biological vs WITHOUT Biological under identical weather and soil parameters.

Outcome: Explains WHY yield increased and mathematically quantifies the exact financial ROI.
